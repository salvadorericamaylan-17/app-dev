## About
**Taxi Driver** (*Mobeom Taeksi*) is a crime-action K-drama that first aired in 2021. It follows a secret taxi company, Rainbow Taxi, that takes revenge for victims when the law fails them.[^1]

> "A taxi that takes revenge on behalf of victims."

---

## Main Characters
1. **Kim Do-gi** (Lee Je-hoon), a taxi driver and former special forces soldier
2. **Jang Seong-cheol** (Kim Eui-sung), the CEO of Rainbow Taxi
3. **Ahn Go-eun** (Pyo Ye-jin), the team's hacker
4. **Choi Kyung-goo** (Kim Do-hoon), the team's mechanic
5. **Park Joo-im** (Bae Yoo-ram), the team's driver and support

## Why I Love It
- Gripping storyline with a new case every arc
- Memorable characters
- Do-gi's many disguises and personalities
- Exciting action scenes
- Strong message about justice for victims
